Product thinking

How I design AI experiences

Start with user intent, not model capability.

AI features become useful when the product knows what the user is trying to do, where they are in the workflow, and how much control they need.

Trust comes from visibility and recovery.

For agentic products, users need to see what the AI is doing, interrupt when needed, and return to a previous state without panic.
