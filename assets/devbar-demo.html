    Setup

    Set up Looop on your Mac.

    Two quick permissions before you can dictate into any app.

    Allow Looop to type into any app
    Needed to detect your cursor and insert text into other apps.
    Allow Looop to use your microphone
    Your voice is sent to a transcription service and discarded after.
    Microphone check

    Speak into your mic.

    The bars on the right should move with your voice. If they don't, switch to a different microphone in System Settings.

    Do the bars react when you talk?
    Listening
    Two shortcuts

    Talk, or translate.

    Tap each one once to feel the difference — they work anywhere on your Mac.

    Transcribe
    Speak. Words appear as-is.
    Press ⌥ to start and stop dictation.
    Translate
    Speak Chinese. English appears.
    Press ⌥ + T to start and stop translation.
    ⌥ conflicts with another app's shortcut. Try a different key.

    Try transcribing

    Write an email — by voice.

    Tap Option and read one of these aloud. Your words land in the email on the right.

    TRY SAYING"Quick project update — first, the design is locked. Second, beta kicks off Monday. Third, we still need legal sign-off."
    Didn't catch that — tap Option and try reading it again.
    To: john@example.com
    Subject: Project update
    Start dictating to fill the email body…
    Try translating

    Speak Chinese, get English.

    Tap Option+T and say a sentence in Chinese. We'll translate it into English.

    TRY SAYING"项目最近进展不错，设计已经定稿，下周一开始公测，最后还差法务签字。"
    Didn't catch that — tap Option+T and try again.
